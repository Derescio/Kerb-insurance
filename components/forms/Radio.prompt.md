One-of-many choice in forms (Yes/No questions, payment frequency).

```jsx
<Radio name="pay" value="monthly" label="Monthly" description="12 payments of £38.20" defaultChecked />
<Radio name="pay" value="annual" label="Annually" description="£432.00 — save £26.40" />
```

- For plan tiers with prices use `CoverageOption` instead.
