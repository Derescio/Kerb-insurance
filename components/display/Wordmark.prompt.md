The Kerb brand wordmark — use in app headers, nav bars, footers and splash screens wherever the brand appears.

```jsx
<Wordmark size={28} />
<Wordmark tone="inverse" size={40} descriptor="car insurance" />
```

- `tone="inverse"` switches the full-stop to Marker yellow on dark.
- Minimum size 18px. Never recolor the letters outside ink / white.
