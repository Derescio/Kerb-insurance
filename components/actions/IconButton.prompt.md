Icon-only circular button for toolbars, headers and close actions.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="arrow-left" label="Back" />
<IconButton icon="bell" label="Notifications" variant="secondary" />
<IconButton icon="x" label="Close" size="sm" />
```

- `inverse` for use on evergreen surfaces. Min 44px hit area on mobile — use `lg` or pad the wrapper.
