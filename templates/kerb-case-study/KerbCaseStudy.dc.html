02 · Approach

Design principles

01

Plain English

Insurance terms stay, but each one is explained where it appears.

02

Show what's next

Every status says what happens next, how long it takes and whether you need to act.

03

One clear action

Only one button per screen uses the yellow accent: the step that matters most.
